RollingGo Partner Center is your single entry point for integrating and distributing RollingGo's travel MCP products. Whether you're embedding live travel search into an enterprise platform, building a developer application, monetizing an audience as a KOL affiliate, or setting up a personal AI travel assistant, Partner Center gives you the credentials, tools, and distribution links you need — all activated the moment you submit your application.

## Partner Types

RollingGo Partner Center supports four distinct partner types, each designed for a specific use case and integration depth.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Enterprise" icon="building" href="/partners/enterprise">
    Integrate RollingGo travel MCP products directly into your business platform. Receive an API Key and MCP configuration parameters for server-side or white-label deployments.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Developer" icon="code" href="/partners/developer">
    Build applications and services on top of RollingGo's travel APIs. Receive an API Key to start calling endpoints immediately after registration.
  </Card>
  <Card title="KOL" icon="share-nodes" href="/partners/kol">
    Distribute RollingGo travel products to your audience as an affiliate. Receive a branded H5 distribution link you can share across social channels and content platforms.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Personal" icon="user" href="/partners/personal">
    Connect RollingGo's travel MCP products to your personal AI assistant (such as Claude or Cursor). Receive a ready-to-use config package with install commands — no account required.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Instant Approval

<Note>
  Every application submitted through RollingGo Partner Center is **auto-approved with zero manual review**. The moment you click **Submit**, your credentials are activated and displayed on screen. There is no waiting period, no approval queue, and no follow-up required.
</Note>

## Region Binding

When you register, you select one of two regions. This choice determines your dashboard domain, billing currency, and email routing — and it is permanent once submitted.

| Region | Domain | Currency |
|---|---|---|
| China Mainland | [rollinggo.cn](https://rollinggo.cn) | CNY (人民币) |
| International / HK / Macau / Taiwan | [rollinggo.ai](https://rollinggo.ai) | USD |

Select **China Mainland** if your operations, end users, or payment flows are primarily within mainland China. Select **International** for all other regions, including Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan. If you need to operate in both regions, you must register separate partner accounts for each.
