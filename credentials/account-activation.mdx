Enterprise, Developer, and KOL partner accounts are activated the moment you submit your application — there is no review wait time and no manual approval step. Your dashboard is ready to use within seconds of submission.

## How Activation Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Form Submitted">
    As soon as you submit your partner application, RollingGo automatically creates your Partner Center account in the background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Status Set to Active">
    Your account status is immediately set to **Active**. No manual review is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Welcome Email Sent">
    An email containing your initial password is sent to the address you provided during registration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Logging In for the First Time

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your confirmation email">
    Look for an email from **partner@rollinggo.ai** (or **partner@rollinggo.cn** for China Mainland accounts) in your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate your initial password">
    Find the temporary initial password included in the email body.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Visit your regional Partner Center">
    Open the dashboard URL that matches your region:

    | Region | Dashboard URL |
    |---|---|
    | China Mainland | https://partner.rollinggo.cn |
    | International | https://partner.rollinggo.ai |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Enter your registered email address and the initial password from the email, then click **Log In**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    You are prompted to change your password on first login. Choose a strong, unique password before continuing to your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Change your password immediately after your first login. Use a password that is at least 12 characters long and unique to your RollingGo account.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I didn't receive the activation email">
    Check your spam or junk folder first — automated emails occasionally get filtered. Allow **2–3 minutes** for delivery after submitting your application. Also confirm that the email address you registered with is spelled correctly. If the problem persists, contact support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see 'This email is already registered — please log in to your existing account'">
    This message appears when the email address you entered during registration is already linked to a Partner Center account. Log in to your existing account at your regional dashboard URL, or use the **Forgot password** link if you no longer have access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password">
    Click the **Forgot password** link on the login page of your regional Partner Center. Enter your registered email address, and you will receive a password-reset link. Follow the instructions in that email to set a new password.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  **Personal users do not have a Partner Center account.** Your configuration resources are delivered to your email, but no dashboard login is created for personal registrations. The steps above apply only to Enterprise, Developer, and KOL partners.
</Note>
