Your H5 distribution link is a personalized URL that tracks every purchase made through your promotion and attributes it directly to your account. It is displayed immediately after you submit your KOL application — bookmark it or copy it to your distribution toolkit straight away.

## What Your Link Looks Like

Your link embeds your unique KOL ID as a `ref` parameter so that all bookings are tied to your account automatically.

```
https://h5.rollinggo.ai/trip?ref=YOUR_KOL_ID
```

<Note>
  If you are a **China Mainland partner**, your link uses the `.cn` domain:

  ```
  https://h5.rollinggo.cn/trip?ref=YOUR_KOL_ID
  ```
</Note>

## Where to Find Your Link

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Success Screen" icon="circle-check">
    Your distribution link appears on the confirmation screen immediately after you submit your KOL application. Copy it before navigating away.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Partner Center Dashboard" icon="link">
    Go to **Distribution → My Links** in your Partner Center dashboard to view, copy, or manage your links at any time.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Share Your Link

You can distribute your H5 link through multiple channels depending on where your audience is.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Direct Link">
    Paste your link directly into social media bios, posts, and stories. It works anywhere a clickable URL is supported — Instagram bio, X profile, Weibo description, or a Xiaohongshu note.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="QR Code">
    Generate a QR code from your Partner Center dashboard under **Distribution → My Links**. Use the QR code in offline and print materials such as flyers, event handouts, and packaging inserts.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mini-Program Path">
    Integrate your link as a page path inside your WeChat mini-program or other mini-program environment. This lets mobile users jump directly into the RollingGo booking flow without leaving your program.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Attribution and Commissions

Every booking completed through your distribution link is attributed to your account. Commissions are calculated based on **confirmed bookings** — not clicks or page views — and are reflected in the earnings section of your Partner Center dashboard.

<Tip>
  Add UTM parameters or use the dashboard's built-in campaign tagging to track performance across different platforms. For example, append `&utm_source=xiaohongshu` to your link for traffic coming from Xiaohongshu, so you can compare channel performance in your dashboard analytics.
</Tip>
