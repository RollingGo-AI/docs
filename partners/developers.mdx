# Understand the Partner Program in 1 Minute

## How It Works

1. **[Register as a Partner](https://partner.rollinggo.cn/register)**: Complete your registration and get your API key. As a Partner, you’ll immediately access our comprehensive product suite.
2. **Access and Distribute Products**: Embed our hotel MCP service into your AI applications, agents, or client tools.
3. **Earn Commissions**: Users search and complete hotel bookings through your application. You receive a commission on every successful booking.

---

## 🛠️ Developer Integration Flow

1. **Obtain API Key**: Register under the Developer track on Partner 2.0 to get your API Key (`mcp_...`).
2. **Configure Markup Rules**: In the Partner Center dashboard, configure your customized markup percentage per destination country/region.
3. **Connect MCP Tools**: Pass the Key in `Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY` when calling `searchHotels` and `getHotelDetail`.
4. **Display Results**: When presenting hotel recommendations to your users, return the provided `bookingUrl` containing your tracking parameters.

---

## 📊 Analytics and Payouts

* **Dashboard Tracking**: Monitor API call volume, conversion rate, total GMV, and accumulated commission in real time.
* **Settlement**: Commissions are settled automatically following guest check-out. Withdraw funds directly to your verified bank account.
