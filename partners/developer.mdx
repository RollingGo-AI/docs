The Developer partner path is built for speed. If you're an individual developer or part of a small agile team building AI agents, travel mini-programs, smart assistants, or any application that needs travel search and booking capabilities, this is your fastest route to a live API Key. Submit the short form and your credentials appear on screen instantly — no review queue, no waiting.

## Onboarding Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Developer card">
    Go to the [RollingGo Partner Center](https://partner.rollinggo.ai) homepage and click the **Developer** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your region">
    Choose **China Mainland** or **International**. This selection is permanent and determines your service endpoint and billing currency.

    | Region | Endpoint | Currency |
    |---|---|---|
    | China Mainland | rollinggo.cn | CNY |
    | International | rollinggo.ai | USD |

    <Warning>Region selection is permanent and cannot be changed after submission.</Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill out the Developer form">
    The Developer form is intentionally brief. You only need to provide:

    - **Intended use case** — describe what you're building. Use the quick-fill presets to speed things up (see tip below).
    - **Contact person name**
    - **Mobile phone** — include your country code (e.g. +86, +1)
    - **Email address** — this becomes your Partner Center login username
    - **Email OTP** — click **Send Code** and enter the 6-digit code sent to your email

    <Tip>
      Use the **quick-fill presets** in the use case field to populate a description in one click. Available presets include **"Travel mini-program developer"** and **"AI travel smart assistant"** — you can edit the filled text afterward to add specifics.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and retrieve your API Key">
    Click **Submit**. Your **API Key** appears on screen immediately. Copy it and store it securely — treat it like a password and never commit it to public source code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password from email">
    Check your registered email inbox for a message from RollingGo Partner Center containing your **initial account password**. Use it to access the dashboard whenever you need to manage your credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The Developer path is designed for individual developers and small teams moving fast. If you represent a larger organization integrating RollingGo for employees or external clients, the [Enterprise path](/partners/enterprise) offers additional fields for business verification and higher-volume use cases.
</Info>
