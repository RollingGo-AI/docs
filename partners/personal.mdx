The Personal path lets you add RollingGo's travel booking Skills directly into the AI tools you already use — without creating a Partner Center account. Fill out a short questionnaire, and you receive a ready-to-use configuration package along with copy-paste install commands right on screen. A download link for the full resource bundle is also sent to your email. At runtime, you authenticate with your own phone number or email via OAuth — there's no static API Key involved.

## Setup Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Personal card">
    Go to the [RollingGo Partner Center](https://partner.rollinggo.ai) homepage and click the **Personal** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your region">
    Choose **China Mainland** or **International**. This determines which service node you connect to and which AI tools appear in the next step.

    | Region | Endpoint | Currency |
    |---|---|---|
    | China Mainland | rollinggo.cn | CNY |
    | International | rollinggo.ai | USD |

    <Warning>Region selection is permanent for this configuration package. If you later need a different region, you'll need to generate a new package.</Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill out the quick questionnaire">
    Answer three short questions to generate your personalized config package:

    - **AI tools you use** — multi-select dropdown listing the tools available for your chosen region (see the [supported tools table](#supported-ai-tools) below). Select everything you want to configure now.
    - **Booking preference** — choose one:
      - **Price-focused** — prioritize the lowest available fare or rate
      - **AI smart recommendation** — let the model weigh price, convenience, and reviews
      - **Brand-name platform** — prefer results from recognized name-brand suppliers
    - **Email address** — used only to send your download resource. This is **not** a login — no account is created.
    - **Email OTP** — click **Send Code** and enter the 6-digit code delivered to your email to confirm your address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and retrieve your config package">
    Click **Submit**. Your **configuration package** and **install commands** appear on screen immediately, tailored to the tools and preferences you selected. Follow the on-screen instructions to paste the config into each tool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the full resource bundle from email">
    Check your inbox for a message from RollingGo Partner Center. It contains a **download link** for the complete resource bundle, including config files, a setup guide, and troubleshooting tips.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The Personal path does **not** create a Partner Center account. You authenticate at runtime using OAuth with your phone number or email — no static API Key is issued or required. If you later need API access to build an integration, or want to distribute travel products as an affiliate, register separately as a [Developer](/partners/developer) or [KOL](/partners/kol) partner.
</Note>
