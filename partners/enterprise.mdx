Enterprise partners are organizations — businesses of any size — that want to embed RollingGo's travel MCP products directly into internal tools, employee portals, or client-facing applications. Once you submit the form, your account is activated immediately: your API Key and MCP connection parameters appear on screen, and your initial login password arrives by email. No waiting, no manual review.

## Onboarding Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Enterprise card">
    Go to the [RollingGo Partner Center](https://partner.rollinggo.ai) homepage and click the **Enterprise** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your region">
    Choose **China Mainland** or **International**. This binding is permanent and determines both your service endpoint and billing currency.

    | Region | Endpoint | Currency |
    |---|---|---|
    | China Mainland | rollinggo.cn | CNY |
    | International | rollinggo.ai | USD |

    <Warning>Region selection is permanent. Choose carefully — you cannot change it after submission.</Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill out the Enterprise form">
    Complete all required fields in the form. See the [field reference table](#field-reference) below for details on each field.

    - **Company legal name**
    - **Unified Social Credit Code** (企业统一社会信用代码)
    - **Existing online product** — whether you currently have a live product (Yes / No)
    - **Estimated daily request volume** — your expected API calls per day
    - **Application description** — a brief overview of your intended use case or functionality
    - **Travel industry** — whether your core business is in the travel sector (Yes / No)
    - **Contact person name**
    - **Mobile phone** — include your country code (e.g. +86, +1)
    - **Email address** — this becomes your Partner Center login username
    - **Email OTP** — click **Send Code** and enter the 6-digit code delivered to your email
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and retrieve your credentials">
    Click **Submit**. Your **API Key** and **MCP connection parameters** are displayed on screen immediately. Copy and store them somewhere safe before navigating away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password from email">
    Check the inbox of your registered email address for a message from RollingGo Partner Center. It contains your **initial account password**. Use it to log in to the dashboard at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Field Reference

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Company Legal Name | Full registered business name as it appears on official documents |
| Social Credit Code | 18-character unified business identifier (统一社会信用代码) |
| Online Product | Whether you currently have a live, publicly accessible product |
| Daily Request Volume | Estimated number of API calls your integration will make per day |
| App Description | Brief description of your use case and the functionality you plan to build |
| Travel Industry | Whether travel services are your core business vertical |
| Contact Name | Full name of the primary point of contact at your organization |
| Mobile Phone | Contact phone number, including international country code (e.g. +86, +1) |
| Email | The address used as your Partner Center login username — must be a real, accessible inbox |
| Email OTP | 6-digit one-time code sent to your email address — click **Send Code** to request it, then enter it to verify your inbox |

<Note>
  Your email address becomes your permanent Partner Center login username. Keep the initial password email in a safe place — you can reset your password from the dashboard at any time, but you'll need access to this email for OTP verification.
</Note>
