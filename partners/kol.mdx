The KOL / Affiliate partner path is designed for content creators, influencers, and channel operators who want to distribute RollingGo travel products to their audience and earn on every booking they drive. Whether you operate on short video platforms, lifestyle communities, or private WeChat groups, you get a personalized H5 distribution link the moment you submit the form. No manual review, no delay — your account is active immediately and your initial password arrives by email.

## How It Works

Your personalized H5 link is the core of your affiliate operation. Share it anywhere your audience is — as a clickable URL, embedded in a mini-program, or turned into a QR code. Every booking completed through your link is attributed to your channel, and earnings are settled through your Partner Center account on the configured payout schedule.

## Onboarding Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the KOL / Affiliate card">
    Go to the [RollingGo Partner Center](https://partner.rollinggo.ai) homepage and click the **KOL / Affiliate** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your region">
    Choose **China Mainland** or **International**. This binding is permanent and determines your service endpoint and settlement currency.

    | Region | Endpoint | Currency |
    |---|---|---|
    | China Mainland | rollinggo.cn | CNY |
    | International | rollinggo.ai | USD |

    <Warning>Region selection is permanent. You cannot change it after submission.</Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill out the KOL form">
    Provide the following details about your channel and contact information:

    - **Primary promotion platforms** — multi-select from Xiaohongshu, Douyin, WeChat, Bilibili, and Other. See the [platform reference table](#supported-platforms) below.
    - **Homepage link / follower count** — a link to your main profile and your approximate follower or subscriber count
    - **Contact person name**
    - **Mobile phone** — include your country code (e.g. +86, +1)
    - **Email address** — this becomes your Partner Center login username
    - **Email OTP** — click **Send Code** and enter the 6-digit code delivered to your email
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and retrieve your distribution link">
    Click **Submit**. Your **personalized H5 distribution link** is displayed on screen immediately. Copy it and start sharing — it's live right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password from email">
    Check your registered email inbox for a message from RollingGo Partner Center containing your **initial account password**. Use it to log in to the dashboard to track conversions and manage payouts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported Platforms

Select every platform where you actively promote content. Selecting more platforms doesn't affect your link — it helps RollingGo provide better channel-specific resources and support.

| Platform | Notes |
|---|---|
| Xiaohongshu (Little Red Book) | Great for lifestyle and travel content — image posts, travel diaries, and recommendations |
| Douyin (TikTok China) | Short video and live stream promotions with high purchase intent |
| WeChat | Private domain traffic via groups, Moments, and official accounts |
| Bilibili | Long-form video, travel vlogs, and niche interest communities |
| Other | Any additional channel — enter the URL or a brief description when prompted |
