## Become a RollingGo Partner at $0 Cost and Get Rewarded for Every Share

# Learn About the Partner Program in One Minute

## How It Works

1. **[Register as a Partner](https://partner.rollinggo.cn/register)**: Complete the registration process in 1-3 minutes and enter the Partner Center.
2. **Obtain Your Promotion Link**: In the Partner Center, navigate to the **Promotion** page to copy your exclusive distribution link or download promotional materials.
3. **Share and Earn**: Share your link via social media, chat groups, blogs, or newsletters. When users make a reservation through your link, you earn a commission on the transaction.

---

## 💡 Promotion Scenarios

* **Social Channels (WeChat, Xiaohongshu, Weibo, X)**: Include your unique link in post comments, bio links, or dedicated travel recommendation posts.
* **Travel Blogs & Guides**: Add your link at the end of travel itineraries with a call to action: "Book the recommended hotel directly here."
* **Community Groups**: Share seasonal weekend hotel deals and exclusive group discounts.

---

## 💰 Commission Settlement

* **Tracking**: The system tracks visitors through your Channel ID with a 30-day attribution window.
* **Payout**: Once the guest completes their stay and checks out, commissions move from pending to withdrawable within 7 business days.
* **Withdrawals**: Flexible bank transfers and digital payments supported directly from the Partner Center.
