# RollingGo Website & Portal Changelog

The RollingGo official portal and developer center are continuously updated to deliver modern documentation, distribution tools, and developer support worldwide.

---

## 2026-10-22

### 📚 Brand-New Mintlify Documentation Center Live

* **Full Bilingual Documentation Architecture**:
  * Upgraded documentation system powered by Mintlify, supporting seamless English and Chinese toggling.
* **Streamlined URL & File Structure**:
  * Refactored all paths using standard kebab-case naming, backed by comprehensive 301 permanent redirects for zero dead links.
* **Sidebar Interaction Upgrade**:
  * Added semantic vector icons to all navigation items and moved the language switcher to the prominent top of the sidebar.
* **Top Navigation & Direct Onboarding**:
  * Integrated direct access back to official portals and prominent CTA buttons routing straight to Partner registration.

---

## 2026-10-14

### 💼 Partner Portal & News Center Released

* **Partner & Creator Distribution Center**:
  * Launched the Partner portal allowing collaborators to configure wholesale markups, generate distribution links, and track commissions.
* **News & Industry Insights**:
  * Introduced dedicated updates and insights channel covering Model Context Protocol (MCP), AI Agent travel practices, and product releases.

---

## 2026-09-30

### 🌐 Official Global Portal Launched

* **Global Portal `global.rollinggo.store` Live**:
  * Tailored for international developers and global travel platforms with complete English coverage and multi-currency showcases.
* **Multi-Device Responsive Optimization**:
  * Enhanced layout, typography, and asset loading across desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.

---

## 2026-08-15

### 🚀 Homepage Redesign & Solutions Showcase

* **Brand Visual Refresh**:

* **Vertical Solutions Showcase**:
