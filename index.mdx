## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Your Partner Type">
    Visit the Partner Center homepage and select the card that matches your profile: Enterprise, Developer, KOL, or Personal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Region">
    Choose **China Mainland** (billed in CNY, domain `rollinggo.cn`) or **International / HK / Macau / Taiwan** (billed in USD, domain `rollinggo.ai`). This binding is permanent after submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Your Application">
    Fill in the short form for your partner type. All fields are validated in real time. Confirm your email with a 6-digit OTP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Credentials Instantly">
    Your API Key, distribution link, or configuration package is displayed on screen the moment you submit — no waiting, no manual review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All partner types are approved automatically. Enterprise and Developer partners also receive their initial account password by email.
</Note>
