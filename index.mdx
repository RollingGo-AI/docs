## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Your Partner Type">
    Select the path that matches your requirements: Enterprise, Developer, KOL, or Personal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Region">
    Choose **China Mainland** (billed in CNY, domain `rollinggo.cn`) or **International / Global** (billed in USD, domain `rollinggo.ai`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Your Application">
    Complete the one-minute registration form with automated real-time verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Instant Access & Credentials">
    Receive your API Key, distribution link, or agent configuration package immediately on screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All partner tiers feature **instant automated onboarding**. Enterprise and Developer partners will also receive their dashboard login credentials via email.
</Note>
