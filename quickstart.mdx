Getting access to RollingGo's travel MCP products takes five steps and under five minutes. You choose your partner type, lock in your region, fill out a short form, and verify your email — then your credentials appear on screen the instant you submit. No approval queue, no waiting, no follow-up required.

## Registration Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Your Partner Type">
    Go to the [Partner Center homepage](https://rollinggo.ai) and click the card that matches your use case: **Enterprise**, **Developer**, **KOL**, or **Personal**. Each card opens the registration form for that partner type. If you're unsure which type fits you, see the [Introduction](/introduction) for a breakdown of each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Region">
    Choose either **China Mainland** (`rollinggo.cn` / CNY) or **International** (`rollinggo.ai` / USD). This binding is permanent — it determines your dashboard domain, billing currency, and email routing for the lifetime of the account. Pick carefully before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill In the Registration Form">
    Complete the registration form for your partner type. The fields vary depending on the type you selected — refer to the individual partner pages for a full breakdown of required information. Common fields include your name, organization (where applicable), contact email, and intended use case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email with a 6-Digit OTP">
    Enter the 6-digit one-time passcode sent to the email address you provided. Check your inbox (and spam folder if needed) for a message from `partner@rollinggo.cn` or `partner@rollinggo.ai` depending on the region you selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and Receive Your Credentials">
    Click **Submit**. Your application is auto-approved instantly. Your credentials — API Key, H5 distribution link, or config package — appear on screen immediately. Save them somewhere secure before navigating away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You Receive

Each partner type receives a different credential upon activation. The table below summarizes the outcome for each.

| Partner Type | Credential Received | Account Created? |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | API Key + MCP configuration parameters | Yes — immediately activated |
| Developer | API Key | Yes — immediately activated |
| KOL | H5 Distribution Link | Yes — immediately activated |
| Personal | Config package + install commands | No |

<Tip>
  **Enterprise and Developer partners:** after submitting, check your inbox for a welcome email containing your initial account password. You'll need this to log in to your dashboard for the first time.
</Tip>
