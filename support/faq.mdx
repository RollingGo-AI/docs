Whether you're signing up for the first time or managing an existing account, these FAQs cover the questions partners ask most often — from approval timelines and credential retrieval to region rules and partner-type differences.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="How long does approval take?">
    All applications are approved instantly. The moment you submit your registration form, your credentials — API Key, H5 link, or configuration package — are displayed directly on screen. There is no waiting period and no manual review step.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change my region after submitting?">
    No. Your region binding — **China Mainland** or **International** — is permanent and cannot be changed after you submit your application. If you need to operate in a different region, register a new partner account using a different email address.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I didn't receive my initial password email. What should I do?">
    Start with these steps in order:

    1. Check your **spam or junk** folder.
    2. Wait **2–3 minutes** — delivery can be slightly delayed.
    3. Confirm you're checking the inbox for the exact email address you registered with.
    4. Check the sender address:
       - **China Mainland** accounts receive email from `partner@rollinggo.cn`
       - **International** accounts receive email from `partner@rollinggo.ai`

    If you still haven't received the email after following these steps, use the **Forgot Password** link on the login page to set a new password directly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Enterprise and Developer?">
    Both partner types receive an API Key and instant account activation. The key differences are:

    | | Enterprise | Developer |
    |---|---|---|
    | **Designed for** | Organizations integrating travel products for employees or clients | Individual developers or small teams building AI agents and travel apps |
    | **Registration requires** | Legal company name and 18-character Social Credit Code | Use-case description (shorter form) |
    | **Account type** | Company account | Individual / team account |

    If you're an independent developer, choose **Developer**. If you're registering on behalf of a company, choose **Enterprise**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the Personal path create an account I can log in to?">
    No. The Personal path is account-free. After you submit your details, you receive a **configuration package and install commands** for your AI tools. Authentication at runtime uses OAuth with your phone number or email — no dashboard login is involved.

    If you later need API access or distribution capabilities, you can register separately as a **Developer** or **KOL** using a different email address.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple partner types under the same email?">
    No. Each email address can only be registered once across all partner types. If you need both Developer and KOL capabilities, register each account with a separate email address.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I find my API Key after leaving the success screen?">
    Log in to your Partner Center dashboard and navigate to **Settings → API Keys**. Your key is listed there.

    If you can no longer access your dashboard, use the **Forgot Password** flow to regain access, then retrieve your key from the same settings page. You can also generate a replacement key from that page if needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What currencies are supported for billing and payouts?">
    Currency is determined by your region selection at registration and cannot be changed afterward:

    - **China Mainland** accounts use **CNY (人民币)**
    - **International** accounts use **USD**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I reset my password?">
    Visit your region's Partner Center login page and click **Forgot Password**. Enter your registered email address and follow the reset link in the email that arrives. If you don't see it within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

    - China Mainland login: `partner.rollinggo.cn`
    - International login: `partner.rollinggo.ai`
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who do I contact for support?">
    Reach the Partner Center support team by email:

    - **International:** `partner@rollinggo.ai`
    - **China Mainland:** `partner@rollinggo.cn`

    Include your **registered email address** and **partner type** in your message so the team can locate your account quickly.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
