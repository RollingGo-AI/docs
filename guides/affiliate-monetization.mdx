As a KOL partner, you earn a commission on every confirmed booking made through your personalized H5 distribution link. RollingGo tracks every click, booking start, and completed purchase back to your link automatically — no pixel setup or third-party tracking required. This guide walks you through sharing your link effectively, reading your performance data in Partner Center, and understanding how and when you get paid.

## Your Distribution Link

Your H5 distribution link is a unique, mobile-optimized booking page that is pre-attributed to your KOL account. Every booking completed through that URL earns you commission at your agreed rate.

Find your link in **Partner Center → Credentials → Distribution Links** — or follow the direct path at [/credentials/distribution-links](/credentials/distribution-links). Copy it from there whenever you need it; the link does not change unless you explicitly request a reset.

## Tracking Performance

Log in to **Partner Center → Distribution → Analytics** to see a real-time breakdown of how your link is performing.

### Available Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| **Link Clicks** | Total times your H5 link was opened |
| **Booking Starts** | Users who began filling out a booking form |
| **Confirmed Bookings** | Paid, completed bookings attributed to your link |
| **Commission Earned** | Total commission accrued from confirmed bookings |

### Using Campaign Tags

Append a `?campaign=` query parameter to your distribution link to track performance across different posts or platforms side-by-side. For example:

```
https://rollinggo.cn/kol/abc123?campaign=douyin_aug
https://rollinggo.cn/kol/abc123?campaign=xiaohongshu_aug
```

Each tagged variant appears as a separate row in your Analytics dashboard, making it easy to compare which content format or platform drives the most confirmed bookings.

## Commissions and Payouts

### How Commission Is Calculated

Commission is calculated on **confirmed (paid) bookings** attributed to your distribution link. A booking counts as confirmed once payment clears — cancelled or failed transactions are excluded. Your specific commission rate is shown in **Partner Center → Distribution → Earnings**.

### Payout Schedule

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Payment date** | 15th of the following calendar month |
| **Minimum threshold** | Varies by region — see Earnings dashboard |
| **Currency** | CNY for China accounts · USD for International accounts |
| **Payment method** | Bank transfer |

### Requesting a Payout

When your balance meets the minimum threshold, a **Request Payout** button appears in your Earnings dashboard. Clicking it opens an invoicing form where you provide your tax ID and bank account details. You are only asked to fill this in at payout time — no advance setup is required.

<Note>
  Commission rates and minimum payout thresholds are displayed in your Partner Center dashboard under **Distribution → Earnings**. For questions about your specific rate or a payout status, contact the KOL partner team:

  - **International:** partner@rollinggo.ai
  - **China:** partner@rollinggo.cn
</Note>
